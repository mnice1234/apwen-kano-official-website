Who We Are

The Voice of Women in Engineering Across Kano State

Founded nationally in 1982 and inaugurated as a proud division of the Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE), the APWEN Kano Chapter brings together practicing female engineers across ministries, manufacturing industries, telecommunications, construction firms, and universities.

Under the leadership of Engr. Safiya Aliyu Mahmoud, MNSE, our chapter actively bridges the gender gap in technical fields while contributing professional expertise to Kano State's infrastructure development.

Constitutional Aims & Mandate

Our Aims & Strategic Objectives

Primary Constitutional Mandate

To provide a vibrant forum for female engineers in Kano State to share technical knowledge, advance professionally to the pinnacle of engineering leadership, advocate for inclusive infrastructure policies, and inspire the girl-child in Northern Nigeria to embrace Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM).

How We Execute

Our 4-Pillar Intervention Model

01. Catch Them Young

Interactive STEM demonstrations in junior and senior secondary schools across Kano before subject selection.

02. Equip & Sponsor

Providing scholarships, textbooks, solar kits, and science lab apparatus to remove financial barriers.

03. Bridge Industry

Securing SIWES industrial placements, factory tours, and technical mentorship for undergraduate women.

04. Certify & Lead

Guiding graduate engineers through COREN interviews and advocating for female representation in executive roles.

COREN & NSE Ethical Code

Our Way of Conduct & Professional Ethics

"To hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public, and to lift every sister engineer as we climb."

1

Integrity & Public Safety

Zero tolerance for substandard designs, structural compromise, or falsification of engineering reports. Public safety always supersedes commercial or personal pressure.

2

Competence & CPD

Members undertake engineering assignments strictly within their areas of certified competence and engage in Continuing Professional Development (CPD) to stay current.

3

Environmental Stewardship

Championing sustainable construction, clean water protection, waste reduction, and renewable energy solutions across Kano's urban and rural ecosystems.

4

Gender Equity & Respect

Maintaining safe, respectful, harassment-free workplaces and sites where women engineers are evaluated strictly on technical merit, skill, and leadership.

5

Transparency in Public Works

Upholding honest procurement, accurate billing of quantities, and transparent project supervision in all government and private infrastructure contracts.

6

Sisterhood & Mentorship

Senior Fellows and Corporate Members actively mentor graduate and student engineers, fostering unity, ethical discipline, and mutual career support.
